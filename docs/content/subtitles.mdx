VitaMediaDeck renders embedded text subtitles across the same local/remote
stream factory as video and audio.

## Supported formats

SubRip (`.srt`), ASS/SSA, WebVTT, MOV text (MP4 timed text), plain text, and
MicroDVD cues — up to 16 tracks, runtime selectable including an explicit
**Off** choice.

## Rendering

- UTF-8 subtitle layout wraps at codepoint boundaries, including text without
  spaces.
- Cues are limited to one through four lines and never split a multibyte
  character when truncating with an ellipsis.
- Western and Cyrillic runs use exact-size Inter Medium or SemiBold; Japanese,
  Chinese, and Korean runs use their native PS Vita system PGFs. The fully
  native system-font option remains available for all runs.

## Style controls

The **Settings > Subtitles** tab controls:

- font;
- foreground and background colors;
- small/medium/large text size;
- 60/75/88/96 percent maximum width;
- one-to-four minimum and maximum lines;
- four vertical positions.

A live preview renders Western, Cyrillic, Japanese, Chinese, and Korean samples.
Inter is rasterized from independent exact-size instances; Japanese, Chinese,
and Korean glyphs come from the matching native system PGFs. Subtitle settings
persist through a checksummed settings record with V1/V2/V3 migration.

## Activation

Subtitle activation never opens a full-screen loading scene. The first cursor
open and all later seeks run as serial requests on one persistent worker while
the R panel stays responsive and shows **Changing...** or a retryable failure.

- The old cue disappears immediately, and read-ahead stops at a small cue limit
  or short future-time horizon.
- A request that does not complete within five seconds becomes a retryable
  failure instead of trapping the UI in **Changing...**.
- Selecting the same failed or pending track and pressing Cross restarts the
  request.
- Circle and the L1 navigation remain available throughout the operation.

> [!NOTE]
> Bitmap subtitles such as PGS and VobSub may be preserved by the Transcoder
> but are not rendered by the current app.
