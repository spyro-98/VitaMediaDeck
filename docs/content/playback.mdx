VitaMediaDeck targets seekable H.264 video in MP4/M4V/MOV or Matroska
containers, with optional mono/stereo AAC and embedded UTF-8 text subtitles.
Local audio detection includes MP3, M4A, AAC, and WAV. Exact compatibility
still depends on the selected decoder and the source media.

## Decoder pipeline

1. A local file or authenticated protocol backend returns independent, seekable
   byte cursors.
2. FFmpeg custom AVIO demuxes H.264 video and AAC audio from the same
   container.
3. `h264_vita` submits access units to the Vita hardware decoder.
4. A software H.264 context is used only if the hardware path cannot open.
5. AAC is decoded through the Vita audio decoder and feeds the audio master
   clock.
6. Video frames enter a bounded ready queue with their source PTS.
7. The presenter reorders by PTS, drops frames that are already obsolete, and
   hands NV12 surfaces to GXM/vita2d.
8. Render completion is signaled before a surface is recycled.
9. Stop/seek is cooperative; every worker is joined before input handles,
   decoders, audio ports, or surfaces are destroyed.

### Hardware acceleration

Hardware-decoded NV12 surfaces stay in CDRAM and are sampled directly for the
960×544 display — no per-frame CPU RGBA conversion or resize runs on the normal
hardware path. The PowerVR GPU renders geometry and presentation; the complete
public `h264_vita` decoder backend handles decode. FFmpeg software H.264
remains an automatic fallback.

### Decoder policy

The **Playback** settings tab selects:

- **Auto** — `h264_vita` preferred with FFmpeg software fallback. Default,
  and permits hardware-to-software fallback.
- **HW H.264** — hardware only.
- **SW FFmpeg** — software only.

The player displays an `HW DEC` or `SW DEC` badge, resolution, frame rate, and
stream-reported video bitrate in both the HUD and the right information panel.

## Indexed cue seeking

The decoder consumes the backward keyframe chosen by FFmpeg, suppresses only
the decoded preroll pictures, retains compatible frame pools, and reuses both
demux cursors. It releases playback after one decoded frame or a bounded gate —
seeks do not wait for a multi-frame startup cushion. This applies equally to
startup, live seek, saved-position resume, and AAC track replacement.

## Track switching

- **Audio**: runtime switching across up to 16 AAC tracks. Changes seek the
  existing audio cursor by its stable stream index; track metadata is
  snapshotted from the already-open video demux rather than a separate probing
  cursor.
- **Subtitles**: embedded SubRip, ASS/SSA, WebVTT, MOV text (MP4 timed text),
  plain-text, and MicroDVD cues, up to 16 tracks. See [Subtitles](subtitles).

## Playback history

Each local or remote video remembers its last useful playback position, keyed
by a stable, non-secret per-media ID. If the player opens from that position,
the R1 panel adds **Start from beginning**, which seeks to `00:00`, removes the
saved resume point, and closes the panel. Local paths and non-secret remote
endpoint/path fields produce stable, distinct IDs; credentials are excluded.

## Stream factory contract

The reusable player consumes a `VitaHwStreamFactory`. Every `open` call returns
a new handle containing:

- a blocking `read` operation;
- `SEEK_SET`, `SEEK_CUR`, and `SEEK_END` support;
- exact source size;
- an idempotent close operation;
- independent cursor state.

Multiple handles may exist concurrently for the same file because video, the
selected audio stream, an explicitly requested subtitle stream, and asynchronous
cover extraction demux independently. A backend must return a fresh cursor for
every `open` and must not share mutable offsets between handles.

## Mini-player hand-off

Short Start minimizes supported into the shared mini-player, which renders the
live video surface. Position and the selected audio/subtitle tracks are
preserved when fullscreen is restored. The compact player reopens through the
same HW/SW decoder stack as fullscreen playback — it does not route video
through the legacy audio player. Selecting the same active video in the grid
restores it instead of restarting it.

## Known limitations

- Some recognized containers or codecs may still be rejected when they do not
  meet the active Vita decoder contract.
- Bitmap subtitles such as PGS and VobSub may be preserved by the Transcoder
  but are not rendered by the app.
