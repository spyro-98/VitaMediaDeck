# Country flags

Source: https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags (main, retrieved 2026-09-07).
The upstream project describes its flags as public-domain national symbols,
derived from Wikimedia Commons and country legislation. Other restrictions on
the use of national symbols may apply.

Bundled PNGs are 64-pixel-wide renders of upstream SVGs using librsvg.
They are indexed by ISO country/territory code, with XK as an upstream extension.
They are navigation aids, not a statement about territorial status.
