# VitaMediaDeck icon candidates

The original five concepts explored VitaMediaDeck's earlier Midnight Halo
palette. The Signal / Shell and Spectral Reassembly redesigns add four
theme-specific iterations:

1. `01-wave-ribbon.png` — playback ribbon and media wave
2. `02-media-deck.png` — stacked media cards and play cutout
3. `03-media-portal.png` — circular playback portal
4. `04-network-stream.png` — local/network streams converging into playback
5. `05-waveform-screen.png` — audio waveform resolving into video playback
6. `06-signal-shell.png` — dimensional segmented shell and telemetry aperture
7. `07-signal-shell-flat.png` — flat shell, play core, and cold machine telemetry
8. `08-particle-shell.png` — holographic shell assembled from
   digital-sand particles and fractured telemetry
9. `09-spectral-core.png` — selected optical media core with spectral-white,
   amber, and teal particle reassembly on pitch-black OLED

Each numbered PNG is a PS Vita-ready 128x128 indexed PNG. The `masters/`
directory contains the original first-round artwork; later high-resolution
masters live in `assets/branding/`. Candidate 9 is active at `sce_sys/icon0.png`
and is mirrored to the three legacy branding filenames. Its incomplete optical
ring, dark core, fractured lattice, pitch-black field, and restrained
spectral-white/amber/teal light directly follow the three visual references
selected for the final theme. Playback is implied by material flow rather than
a solid cartoon-style button.

`comparison.png` records the original five-concept review. Candidates 6–9
were produced during the later Signal / Shell pass and are documented by their
numbered previews and full-resolution masters.
